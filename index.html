Field Kit

Offline-first kit for rides, hikes, fishing, and field work. Checklists, notes, and a moon phase that is calculated on your device, no signal needed after the page loads. No signup, no upload. Tide times are entered by hand from your local source, this app does not invent them.

Moon tonight

Calculated from the date using the synodic month (29.53 days). Approximate, good for planning light, not for navigation.
